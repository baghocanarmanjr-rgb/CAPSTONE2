FINAL LIVE TEST CHECKLIST
========================

1. Start Docker Desktop / local Supabase.
2. Start the revised system with START_SYSTEM.ps1.
3. Open PPMP.
4. Confirm a newly created PPMP shows status Draft.
5. Click Review.
6. Verify all PPMP items/details are visible on the read-only review page.
7. Tick the review confirmation and click Mark PPMP ... as Reviewed.
8. Return to PPMP and confirm status is Reviewed.
9. Open Review again, tick approval confirmation, and click Approve PPMP.
10. Confirm PPMP status is Approved.
11. Open APP. Confirm only Approved PPMPs without an APP are listed.
12. Select an Approved PPMP. The exact selected record must load without the red "Unable to load" error.
13. Change to a different Source PPMP and verify the entire preview changes to the selected record.
14. Generate APP and verify its source PPMP, item data, and total match the preview.
15. Confirm the PPMP remains Approved after APP generation/approval and PR creation/approval.
16. Open PPMP Edit items on a Draft/Reviewed PPMP and verify the modal is centered in the viewport.
17. Save an edit and confirm the PPMP returns to Draft so it must be reviewed and approved again.
18. Confirm a PPMP that already has an APP cannot be edited.
19. Open the login page after signing out. Verify the green promotional panel is inset from the top/bottom, wider, rounded, and the login form is centered in the white workspace.
20. Test tablet/mobile width: green panel hides on small screens and there is no horizontal scrolling.

Expected PPMP workflow:
Draft -> Reviewed -> Approved -> APP
